# Limitations

Public retrieval may fail, pages may change, and authorization evidence may be private or expired. Similarity is only a candidate signal and can produce false links or miss paraphrased copies. The rule-based aggregation is not calibrated probability. The baseline cannot be validated without a defensible labelled dataset. Human annotation, source selection, temporal drift, employer imbalance, and selection bias limit generalization. OCR, PDFs, authenticated sources, and large-scale collection are not implemented.
