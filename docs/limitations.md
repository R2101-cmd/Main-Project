# Limitations

Public retrieval may fail, pages may change, and authorization evidence may be private or expired. Similarity is only a candidate signal and can produce false links or miss paraphrased copies. The rule-based aggregation is not calibrated probability. OCR/PDF support is partial (poster OCR depends on available components), and authenticated or restricted sources are not accessed.

**Automated validation mode:** real-world ground-truth evaluation is unavailable (no adjudicated human labels or confirmed lineage). Automated lineage categories and independence estimates are conservative, machine-generated outputs; similarity-based relationships are not confirmed lineage and unknown relationships are not assumed independent. Descriptive real-data metrics are system outputs only, not accuracy/precision/recall/F1/false-assurance performance against ground truth. Historical human-review artifacts, where present, are preserved but not required for execution. Synthetic and extended synthetic results are controlled implementation checks only and do not establish real-world performance or prove the hypothesis. Missing or unavailable evidence is never treated as fraud.
