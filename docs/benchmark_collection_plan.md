# Benchmark collection plan

## Required sampling strata

Collect real advertisements across: directly supported vacancies, affirmative contradictions, unresolved third-party posts, repost families, copied text, genuinely independent corroboration, established employers with uncertain specific vacancies, inaccessible sources, and stale/expired vacancies.

## Collection fields

Retain stable case IDs, original text or permitted snapshot references, submitted and final URLs, employer/title/location, collection timestamp, source type, publication/deadline dates, redaction status, evidence IDs, lineage signals, reviewer IDs, and label rationale.

## Review design

Freeze a sampling seed and source/employer strata. Have two reviewers independently label a fixed subset without predictions, adjudicate disagreements, and maintain a separate lineage-review subset. Do not force class balance or manufacture contradictions.

## Current blocker

The repository has 145 collected records but only a provisional single-reviewer 31-record subset. Additional records alone will not fix the benchmark; independent annotation, affirmative contradictions, and reviewed lineage relationships are the priority.
