# Annotation guidelines (execution package)

- Record evidence, not assumptions. Keep **employer identity**, **vacancy authorization**, and **fraud outcome** separate.
- Labels: employer identity/vacancy authorization `supported|contradicted|unresolved`; fraud outcome `confirmed_legitimate|confirmed_fraudulent|unresolved` only with defensible basis.
- Evidence states: `supported|contradicted|unverified|unavailable`. Missing/inaccessible evidence is **not** fraud or contradiction.
- Capture per case: case/ad IDs, claims, checked official/ATS URLs, retrieval timestamps, matched/contradicted fields, source authority/lineage, reviewer decision, rationale, confidence, adjudication status (`pending_review|reviewed|disputed|adjudicated|insufficient_evidence`).
- Require evidence references and rationale for non-unresolved decisions. Single-reviewer datasets must be marked provisional; do not claim inter-annotator agreement without independent overlapping reviews.
- Preserve original evidence/annotations; resolve disagreements via adjudication records.
