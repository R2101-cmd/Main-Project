# Remaining limitations

1. **No trusted employer mappings are shipped.** `config/employer_sources_v1.json` intentionally contains an empty `mappings` list because this audit did not establish documentary employer-domain or ATS relationships. Until a mapping with provenance is supplied, all source authority stays unresolved.
2. **No live lineage is adjudicated automatically.** Collected records remain `source_relationship=unknown`, `lineage_status=not_assessed`, and `lineage_review_status=not_assessed`. Similarity results are candidates only. No group IDs or confirmed relationships are manufactured. Consequently, the live C result and final decision will commonly remain unresolved.
3. **The live final decision requires two qualifying independent source groups.** This is a conservative operational sufficiency rule, not a threshold calibrated on an independent real-world dataset. It is stricter than the raw A/B/C aggregation output; the research methods themselves are unchanged.
4. **Freshness is a policy, not a learned estimate.** The 180-day window and date precedence are transparent conservative rules. `validThrough` expiry is used as an explicit end date; a recent retrieval alone does not refresh a posting. Missing, unparsable or future source dates remain unresolved.
5. **Field extraction remains best-effort.** Complex salary ranges, currencies, pay periods, location aliases, multi-location roles, dynamic pages and ambiguous identifiers may be incomparable. Salary is only marked conflicting when currency and pay period are explicit and the amount parses as one value; ranges are deliberately left incomparable.
6. **Retrieval does not bypass access controls.** Login, CAPTCHA, JavaScript-only content and non-public sources remain unavailable or incomplete. No external employer page was fetched during this implementation pass.
7. **Current-version performance has not been measured on an untouched test set.** The historical synthetic Stage 7B numbers refer to an earlier frozen implementation and are not evidence of the present live decision policy’s performance. Real cohorts have no independent authorization ground truth, so real-world accuracy metrics remain unavailable.
8. **AppTest is not a full browser/server deployment test.** It exercised all three mode layouts and submitted a deterministic pasted-text case. Poster OCR dependencies and live external retrieval remain environment-dependent; OCR unit tests passed in the full suite.
9. **Existing worktree changes predated this task.** The initial worktree was already dirty and contained many untracked research files. Those were preserved; the changed-files manifest marks files already modified/untracked at the start.
10. **Some protected artifacts have only current hashes.** Five historical synthetic artifacts (extended 30-case JSONL/manifest and three `controlled_v2` files) and the 3,841-file, 590.7 MB `DatasetCollection/raw` tree were not included in the pre-implementation checksum set. Current hashes are recorded, but no prior hash was available to prove a session-level before/after match. No generator, evaluator or dataset writer was run against them.
