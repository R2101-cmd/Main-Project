# Synthetic benchmark v3

150 deterministic synthetic records, 50 per authored outcome. Five support families contain ten examples each, five contradiction families contain ten each, and ten unresolved families contain five each. Support requires two distinct adjudicated independent groups; contradiction requires two such groups with a consistent material vacancy contradiction; unresolved cases are constructed with dependence, unknown or unadjudicated lineage, source conflict, absent vacancy correspondence, stale or missing dates, unavailable sources or insufficient details. Labels are determined by these constructed evidence conditions, not model outputs, and are not human-reviewed ground truth. Inputs exclude expected labels, scenario names and evaluation rationales. Generated with Python random seed 20261115.
