# Real-world discovery cohort v1

Observed 2026-11-17 from public career pages. Ten records across five employers broaden role-family, source-host, and geography coverage. This is a discovery/sampling cohort only: all labels are blank, all records are evaluation-ineligible, and a page being listed at observation does not establish later availability or authorization. Four Razorpay roles come from its employer-linked Greenhouse account; Okta, Airbnb, Microsoft and BEML are employer-hosted sources. BEML is a multi-post recruitment PDF and may be historical/closed.

`double_review_queue_v1.csv` reserves independent reviewer A/B slots; do not fill both from one reviewer or derive labels from this discovery note. `poster_ocr_eval_v1.csv` records a real official BEML PDF candidate. Downloading its bytes was blocked by this environment's network restrictions, so the project's OCR engine was not run and no OCR score is claimed.

Calibration and final accuracy evaluation remain gated on independent double annotation, adjudication, evidence/lineage review, a frozen group-separated split, and an actual poster image asset.
